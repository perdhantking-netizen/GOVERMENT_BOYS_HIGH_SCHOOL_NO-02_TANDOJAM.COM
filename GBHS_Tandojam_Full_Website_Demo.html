About Our School

A digital front door for school information and services.

G.B.H.S. NO:02 TANDOJAM

Our proposed Smart School Management System brings common school tasks into one place. Authorized staff can manage student records, attendance, notices and academic information, while students can access their own protected information after secure sign-in.

Portal access

Different accounts and permissions for each role help keep school information organized.

  • Admin — users, records and settings
  • Principal — school oversight and notices
  • Teacher — assigned classes, attendance and marks
  • Student — own profile, attendance and results
